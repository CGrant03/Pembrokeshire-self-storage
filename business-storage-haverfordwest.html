Home / Business storage in Haverfordwest

If stock and equipment are taking over your working space, a separate storage container can help keep your premises organised. We offer 20ft × 8ft container storage at Cartlett Office & Storage Park in Haverfordwest for local businesses and tradespeople.

Storage for stock and seasonal equipment

A container can hold boxed business stock, display equipment and seasonal items that do not need to stay at your premises. Before booking, tell us what the goods are, how they are packed and whether they need particular storage conditions. That helps us discuss whether a container is suitable.

Tools and equipment for tradespeople

Builders and other trades often need space between jobs for tools, equipment and materials. Make a list of the items you want to store and check any restrictions in the storage agreement. Do not assume fuels, chemicals, gas cylinders or other hazardous materials are permitted; discuss the items with us first.

Storage rental is for storing agreed belongings. It does not by itself provide permission to run a workshop, receive customers or work from the container.

Plan access around your working day

Think about how often you will collect stock or equipment. Put regularly used items near the entrance and allow a clear route through your stored goods. Check your access arrangements with us before committing to a delivery or collection schedule.

The yard has gated access and CCTV. We confirm availability and the agreed start date before move-in. Access codes follow the required agreement and payment steps; an enquiry alone does not reserve a unit.

Understand the monthly cost

A 20ft container costs £130 plus VAT per month (£156 including VAT). The minimum rental is one month. We confirm your rental arrangements before you book, so please ask about the expected length of storage and any requirements specific to your business.

The yard does not provide insurance for your stock, tools or equipment. Check your business policy or arrange suitable cover for goods kept away from your premises in a storage container.

Choose a container that suits your goods

Our container size guide explains external dimensions, measuring bulky items and planning a useful layout. Internal dimensions and door openings are smaller than the quoted 20ft × 8ft size, so confirm measurements before booking for a large item.

To enquire, tell us your business storage needs, preferred start date and contact details. Our Haverfordwest yard serves customers across Pembrokeshire; check the yard location and directions to decide whether it suits your regular journeys.
