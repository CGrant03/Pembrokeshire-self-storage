Choosing storage is easier when you know the size, the price and what to check before moving in. This guide covers the 20ft × 8ft containers at Pembrokeshire Self Storage in Haverfordwest.

How big is a 20ft container?

Our quoted container size is 20ft long × 8ft wide externally—approximately 6.10m × 2.44m. That is a nominal external footprint of 160 square feet (about 14.9 square metres), not a measurement of usable internal floor space.

The walls, doors and frame reduce the space available inside. Door openings are smaller again. If you need to store a large sofa, assembled wardrobe or bulky piece of equipment, measure it and ask us to confirm the relevant dimensions before booking.

How much will fit?

Capacity depends on the shape and quantity of your belongings, whether furniture can be dismantled and how much aisle space you need. Counting bedrooms alone is not a reliable way to decide. Make an inventory and note the dimensions of your largest items.

  • Group boxes of similar sizes so they stack safely.
  • Place heavy items low down and avoid overloading stacks.
  • Allow access to belongings you will collect regularly.
  • Keep vents clear and avoid packing everything tightly against the walls.
  • Discuss delicate or moisture-sensitive goods with us before booking.

What does it cost?

Our monthly price is £156 including VAT (£130 + VAT), with a one-month minimum. It is a monthly price, not a four-week price. When comparing quotes, check the rental period, VAT, unit size, insurance and any other charges so you are comparing the same thing.

Availability and your start date are confirmed by our team. The first month is paid upfront; monthly Direct Debit is scheduled from month two once bank setup is confirmed. Your agreement and payment link are sent after your enquiry is reviewed.

Ventilation and protecting your belongings

Our containers have additional vents to help reduce condensation. They do not remove the need to pack dry belongings or check whether container storage suits your goods. Dry appliances and furniture before storing, use suitable protective covers and leave ventilation openings unobstructed.

Insurance for your belongings is not included or provided by the yard. Check your existing cover or arrange a policy suitable for items kept in a storage container.
